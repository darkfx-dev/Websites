# Client Content Needed — Photography

The website currently ships **photo-light by design**. No approved photographs
of the **Katargam (Sunday Hub)** outlet were available, so the layout uses
typography, warm colour, subtle texture and an original code-drawn hero
illustration instead of stock or placeholder imagery.

The site is complete and launchable as-is. Supplying the photographs below will
let us add a proper gallery and art-direct the hero and menu sections with real
food photography.

## Important rules for supplied images

- **Only the Katargam / Sunday Hub outlet.** Do **not** send photos from the
  Athwa, Adajan, Honey Park, Chowpatty or any other Mahesh Pav Bhaji branch.
- Confirm each photo is genuinely from this location — never guess from a
  filename.
- You own the rights (your own photos, or a photographer who has granted use).
  Please don't send Google Maps user photos or images downloaded from elsewhere.

## Technical guidance

- **Format:** JPG or PNG straight from the camera/phone is fine — we convert to
  optimized AVIF/WebP during the build.
- **Resolution:** at least ~2000px on the long edge so we can crop cleanly.
- **Orientation:** where noted, a landscape **and** a portrait/square version of
  the same subject gives the most layout flexibility.
- **Naming:** describe the actual content, e.g.
  `katargam-exterior-day.jpg`, `pav-bhaji-cheese.jpg`, `interior-seating.jpg`.

## Where they go

Place approved files in `public/images/` and let us know — we will:

1. Wire them through `next/image` with correct sizes and truthful alt text.
2. Re-enable the **Gallery** section and its navigation link.
3. Art-direct the hero with a real photograph and a text-safe crop.

Until then, the site remains fully functional without any placeholder boxes or
"image coming soon" states.

Questions, answered.

Every bag lists a recommended grind for espresso, pour-over, French press, and AeroPress, along with a suggested dose and ratio for each.

Choose a roast and a cadence — weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Skip, swap, or cancel anytime from your account, no phone calls required.

Direct-trade lots from Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, sourced through relationships our roasters maintain in person, season over season.
